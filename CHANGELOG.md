# Changelog

All notable changes to Playlist Downloader are recorded here. The format follows
Keep a Changelog; the newest version is first.

## [3.0.0] - unreleased

A new app, rebuilt from the ground up. Playlist Downloader 3 is a fresh Qt 6 desktop
application: search YouTube for playlists, videos and channels, browse a playlist's
entries, play them in the built-in browser and download what you pick. The left rail
holds the Search, Playlist, Browser and Downloads pages, with settings and the keyboard
shortcuts at the bottom of it.
